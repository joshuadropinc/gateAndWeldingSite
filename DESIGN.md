## Brand & Style

This design system expresses heavy-duty architectural fabrication and bespoke metalworking. It balances raw structural strength with precise, high-end finishing. The visual language blends architectural precision, industrial geometric rigor, and tactile metalcraft surfaces to evoke uncompromising trust, durability, and premium artisan quality.

### Personality & Tone
- **Unyielding & Heavy-Duty:** Grounded, fortified, and built to survive the elements.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-sharp alignment, deliberate structural grids, and technical clarity.
- **Mastery & Craftsmanship:** Premium custom execution rather than mass-market utility.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for property owners, commercial developers, and general contractors commissioning custom security gates, architectural fencing, and structural mobile welding. The interface must inspire absolute confidence in structural integrity, professional capability, and transparent engineering value.

## Colors

The palette references structural steel, slag-shielded fabrication, torch heat, and high-tensile fasteners. The dark aesthetic establishes an authentic workshop and site-installation presence while ensuring high contrast for safety and functional legibility.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#0F1216`):** Deep iron ground that eliminates glare and establishes an authoritative substrate.
- **Structural Plate / Container 1 (`#1E232A`):** Dark slate primary plate for cards, modules, and navigation sheets.
- **Raised Tooling / Container 2 (`#22252A`):** Subtle tonal step for elevated cards, active rows, and interactive surfaces.
- **Milled Steel Accents (`#334155` and `#64748B`):** Structural borders, secondary text, metadata labels, and technical schematics.

### High-Heat Accents
- **Industrial Amber (`#D97706`):** The primary signal for high-priority calls to action, active toggles, and verified badges.
- **Forge Rust Orange (`#EA580C`):** High-urgency triggers, live dispatch indicators, mobile welding availability badges, and key metric callouts.

### Contrast & Functional Roles
Text on dark containers defaults to `#F8FAFC` for headline authority and `#94A3B8` for supportive body context. Interactive focus states utilize high-contrast amber outlines (`#D97706`) with interior dark offsets to mimic etched metal tooling.

## Typography

Typography pairs mechanical precision with grounded legibility:
- **Headlines & Technical Labels (Space Grotesk):** Engineered glyphs and tight geometric cuts reinforce structural craftsmanship, blueprints, and plate stamping. Uppercase tracking applies across micro-labels, serial badges, and spec lists.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Work Sans):** Neutral, rock-solid proportions maintain legibility under harsh lighting conditions and field device use without distracting from industrial visual assets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on a 12-column rigid grid with clear structural lanes, reflecting architectural blueprints and steel truss arrangements.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container bound at 1440px centered.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Structural Alignment & Layout Philosophy
- Density is calibrated for operational efficiency: high data-density on specification tables and estimates, balanced with structured breathing room around fabrication photography.
- Avoid loose asymmetrical wandering. Elements clamp strictly into the multi-column framework, creating sharp vertical and horizontal alignment planes.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, floating cloud-like shadows in favor of **structural containment, machined seams, and calibrated bevel shadows**.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary ground sits at `#0F1216`. Layered surfaces utilize `#1E232A` (Tier 1) and `#22252A` (Tier 2).
- **Hard Technical Outlines:** Structural borders are set to `1px` or `2px` solid `#334155`. Cards and structural modules rely on these crisp, plate-to-plate seams for division rather than fuzzy drop shadows.
- **Machined Shadow Offset:** When physical elevation is required (modals, dropdown tools, sticky quick-quote panels), utilize a tight, dark offset shadow: `0 4px 0 0 #07090C, 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Welding Torch Glow:** Active states and urgent callouts utilize a localized, high-density amber aura: `0 0 16px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **softened structural industrial edge (`roundedness: 1`)**. Sharp 90-degree corners are milled to minimal corner radii (`4px / 0.25rem` standard, `8px / 0.5rem` large container), echoing CNC-cut sheet metal, plate steel, and heavy tube profiles.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` corner radius.
- **Structural Cards & Viewports:** `8px` corner radius.
- **Chamfered Cuts:** Specific industrial elements (such as technical spec badges and quote callouts) may employ a geometric 45-degree corner clip (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to denote plate fabrication.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary CTA (High-Impact Amber):** Solid `#D97706` background with `#0F1216` heavy text (`Space Grotesk`, semi-bold, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`). Border: `1px solid #F59E0B`. Hover: `#EA580C` with an amber-tinted shadow.
- **Secondary CTA (Milled Steel):** Background `#1E232A`, border `1px solid #64748B`, text `#F8FAFC`. Hover: Border `#94A3B8`, background `#22252A`.
- **Emergency Dispatch / Mobile Welding Trigger:** Background `#EA580C`, high-contrast white text, continuous pulse border animation for active 24/7 service.

### Cards & Heavy-Duty Panels
- Constructed from `#1E232A` slate ground, wrapped in a `1px solid #334155` border.
- Optional header accent: A 2px top stroke in `#64748B` or `#D97706` denoting panel status.
- Card padding remains strict: `1.5rem` on desktop, `1rem` on mobile.

### Badges & Industrial Chips
- Uppercase `label-sm` typography inside a fixed `24px` height container.
- **Spec / Material Grade Badge:** Border `1px solid #334155`, background `#12161B`, text `#94A3B8`.
- **Status / Certified Badge:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #D97706`, text `#F59E0B`.

### Input Fields & Estimation Calculators
- Background `#12161C`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- Focus state: Border shifts instantly to `1.5px solid #D97706` with no soft blur, maintaining an etched line aesthetic.
- Labels utilize `label-md` in Space Grotesk positioned strictly above fields with explicit measurement units (e.g., `GAUGE`, `LINEAR FT`, `ALLOY`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Box is square with a `2px` micro-radius, filled with `#12161C`, outlined by `1px solid #475569`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#D97706`, displaying an authoritative geometric check icon in `#0F1216`.

### Trust Signals & Technical Specs Grid
- Certified AWS (American Welding Society) and structural guarantee indicators rendered with high-contrast etched monochrome badges.
- Technical spec sheets rendered in alternating row bands (`#1E232A` / `#181C22`) with `1px` tabular gridlines in `#2B333E`.
